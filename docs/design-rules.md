# Design rules and balance rules

Two kinds of rules keep ships and weapons consistent. **Design rules** check each ship design on paper.
**Balance rules** check weapons against each other in short test battles between stationary test
platforms.

## Design rules (`npm run designs:audit`)

Every ship in the **Playable** and **Test platforms** sections of the library must pass these rules (a test
enforces it). Ships in the **Experimental** section (demo ships and balance archetypes) are audited but not
held to the rules: tests use them as fixtures.

| Rule | Pass mark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | no errors or warnings | the editor's own check (connections, overlaps, exhaust and radiator clearance, gimbal) |
| Power | production ≥ 1 × combat demand | reactors cover weapons, point defense, gyros, command and hangars |
| Cooling | ≥ 1 × sustained combat heat | (dissipation + heat capacity ÷ `fireTime`) against the heat of the power drawn plus weapon and gyro heat: small ships may lean on their heat capacity for a game of fire, large ones need radiators |
| Ammunition | ≥ 0.9 × `fireTime` per weapon | magazines (and rounds in the guns) give every weapon of a type at least 90 % of a game of fire (`game.fireTime`, 144 s): about one small magazine per gun |
| Delta-v | ≥ 300 m/s | for ships with engines |

Two warnings are not counted: "no weapons" (targets and haulers are unarmed on purpose) and, for test
platforms, "no engines".

`npm run designs:audit -- --fix` brings the bundled designs that break a rule up to it by adding parts in
mirrored pairs: magazines for weapons that are short of ammunition, reactors for power, heat sinks for
cooling. A pair goes on a free attach edge where it adds no layout issue, with the spot chosen close to the
ship's centre and rear. Each pair may block at most 45° more of the turrets' arcs (90° as a last resort).
Armored hulls with no free outer edge get interior hull blocks swapped for magazines instead. The 2026-11-14
pass added magazines to every playable ship except the Spark, Maul and Needle, and two reactors to the
Viper. Five mirror scenarios got larger budgets so their reference fleets still fit.

## Test platforms (`npm run designs:platforms`)

The **Test platforms** library section holds stationary ships, each with one kind of weapon, plus two
targets:

- gatling (one point-defense gatling), gatling 2 (two), flak
- missile launcher (smart missiles), rocket launcher
- autocannon turret, laser turret, cannon turret
- target block, armored target

Each has a fuel tank and a small engine: a ship that can neither move nor fire at ships counts as defeated
and nothing shoots at it. With AI "none" in a scenario a platform has no pilot and stays where it is placed.
Its weapons are in group 1 set to automatic fire, so they engage on their own. The designs are generated by
`src/headless/platforms-cli.ts`.

## Balance rules (`npm run tuning -- tests`)

The tuning tests are scenarios built from the platforms (`src/headless/tuning/tests.ts`; JSON copies in
`src/data/scenarios/tests`, which the scenario editor can open). Each runs over seeded battles with slightly
jittered spawns. The rules are checked over the results:

| Rule | Test | Range | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| One gatling stops one missile | 1 launcher vs 1 gatling, 2.5 km | all stopped in ≥ 90 % of runs | 91 % |
| A second missile has a chance to pass | 2 launchers vs 1 gatling | one gets through in 10–80 % | 44 % |
| Two gatlings stop two missiles | 2 launchers vs 2 gatlings | all stopped in ≥ 90 % | 100 % |
| A third missile has a chance to pass | 3 launchers vs 2 gatlings | one gets through in 10–80 % | 11 % |
| The laser is a little weaker than the autocannon | each turret against an unbreakable target block at 400, 800 and 1100 m for 20 s | laser ÷ autocannon damage 0.80–0.95 | 0.87 |

How the current values were reached:

- **Missile HP** follows point-defense fire (`hp.missile.pdSecondsPerKg` = 0.0029: a smart missile has 189
  HP, a rocket 126). A kill takes whole gatling hits, so this value only matters in steps. The step one hit
  lower would let one gatling stop a single missile every time, but a third missile would almost never get
  past two gatlings. The step one hit higher would have one gatling miss a single missile 18 % of the time.
- **Beam damage** (`weapons.family.beam` = 0.21) puts the laser turret at 0.87 of the autocannon turret. The
  laser does more damage close in; the autocannon does more at long range.

The GA adds the squared misses of these rules to its loss (`rules` in the config: `weight` 2, `runs` 20
per salvo test), so a long run cannot trade them away.
